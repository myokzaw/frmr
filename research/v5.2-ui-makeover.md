# frmr v5.2: UI makeover proposal

**Date:** 2026-10-21
**Author:** design-engineering research pass (no app code changed)
**Status:** Proposal for lead and user review. Nothing here is decided.
**Baseline:**
- v5 shipped on `v5-photo-camera` (`1d4a1dd`).
- v5.1 in progress in two worktrees:
  - `worktrees/camera` (branch `v5.1-camera`): `CameraChrome`, `GlassIconButton`, `ToolIconBar` and `EffectScrubber` exist; the Diary review screen is deleted.
  - `worktrees/restyle` (branch `v5.1-restyle`): so far only a seed fixture and "before" screenshots of Settings.

**Read with:** `CLAUDE.md`, `project.md` §2 and §5–§7, `frmr-v5-spec.md`, `frmr-v5.1-spec.md` (F8–F10), `.claude/skills/ios-camera-orientation/SKILL.md`.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **frmr has no design system yet, only a palette.**
   - There are 49 hard-coded `.font(.system(size:))` calls and zero `@ScaledMetric` or `relativeTo:` uses, so nothing responds to Dynamic Type.
   - Nothing reads Reduce Transparency or Reduce Motion.
   - The selected state is drawn five ways: white pill, teal fill, outlined capsule, teal underline and amber fill.
   - **Highest-impact change:** a small token layer (type roles, spacing, radii, colour roles, one selection style), landed *first* so the parallel agents build on it.
2. **Colour contrast fails in places people look all the time.** Measured against WCAG 2.x:

   | Pairing | Ratio | Where it appears |
   |---|---|---|
   | White on mid-teal | 3.73:1 | "Save to roll" button |
   | Dark-teal tab tint on near-black | 2.62:1 | The "Camera" label on the camera tab |
   | Rust on near-black | 3.46:1 | Destructive rows in dark mode |
   | Burnt orange on cream | 2.72:1 | Character count |
   | Amber on cream | 1.59:1 | Amber must never be text or a thin indicator on cream |

   **Fix:** amber becomes a *fill* with near-black text on it (8.47:1), and every text tint gets a per-scheme value.
3. **The camera's bottom third is still the busiest part of the app.** The v5.1 work clears the WB/EV chips from it. v5.2 goes further:
   - On a 3:4 viewfinder, put the dial and shutter row **on black below the image** instead of over it, as Apple Camera does on tall phones. The picture is then unobstructed, and glass is kept for the few controls that must float.
   - Fall back to the glass overlay on short phones.
4. **The editor asks people to pick a film by name.** The Film and Print chips are text pills (`edit-film-tab.png`).
   - Replace them with a **filmstrip of this photo rendered in each stock**: small thumbnails, the Photos-app "Filters" pattern.
   - Of everything in this list, this is the change most likely to make the film look *feel* like the product.
5. **The Library inverts its own hierarchy.**
   - Entries show only the prompt; the user's own words never appear (`diary-list-cine500T.png`).
   - Strips are a one-column ribbon using about 15% of the width (`library-strips-booth.png`).
   - The Roll grid's first tile bleeds past the left margin (`library-roll-grid.png`).
   - **Proposal:** contact-sheet Roll, excerpt-first Entries, two-column Strips.
6. **There are layout bugs to fix whatever the restyle decides:**
   - Strip screen: Close overlaps the strip, and Save/Share/Archive are cut off at the bottom (`strip-screen-sliders.png`).
   - List content scrolls under the floating tab bar with no edge effect (`diary-list-cine500T.png`).
   - Coach mark: the "Library" bubble is shown on Settings, with its arrow pointing at a slider (`worktrees/restyle/.../before-settings-top-light.png`).
   - The prompt "…that you don't" is shown without its ending "yet?" (`review-ok-prompt-mono400.png`). Either it is truncated, or the screenshot was taken mid-typewriter; verify which.
7. **Glass is used as decoration instead of as a layer.**
   - Apple's guidance: Liquid Glass belongs to controls and navigation floating over content, used sparingly, with no glass on glass, and grouped in a `GlassEffectContainer`.
   - **Proposal:** six glass rules (§4.5), and an upgraded `frmrGlass` with a prominent variant, a Reduce Transparency fallback and a container wrapper.
8. **Nothing in the app says "film" except the pixels.** Five offline signature moments (§6) give frmr a feel of its own, all with Apple frameworks only:
   - a film-advance haptic;
   - a "developing" reveal that plays only during the real wait for the RAW;
   - a contact-sheet Roll with edge print;
   - stock swatches on the dial;
   - a strip that prints out of a slot.
9. **Use system components wherever frmr currently re-creates them, so iOS 26 restyles them for free:**
   - TipKit for the walkthrough (instead of the custom `CoachBubble`/`CoachArrow`);
   - `ContentUnavailableView` for empty states;
   - `Form` with the grouped style for Settings;
   - the native `TabView`, with `tabBarMinimizeBehavior` on iOS 26.
10. **Sequencing:**
    - Step 0: one small tokens PR (Type, Colour roles, Spacing, Radii, Glass) by one agent.
    - Then three parallel tracks: camera/editor/strip, Library/viewers/Settings/walkthrough, and backend (haptics engine, swatch rendering, prompt fix).
    - Then a QA accessibility pass at AX5 Dynamic Type, Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency.

### 2.2 Screen by screen

**Camera: Photo** (`camera-photo-collapsed.png`, `camera-photo-wb-panel.png`, `camera-photo-ev-panel.png`)
- Six stacked layers in the bottom 35%: WB/EV chips, dial, caption, shutter, tab bar, home indicator. Every control has the same weight. v5.1 moves the WB/EV chips to the top right, which is right.
- The dial cuts off at the left edge ("C500" half-clipped) and has no fade mask. The selected cell is an outlined capsule, a style used nowhere else.
- The mono caption "Mono 3200" repeats the code above it (v5.1 removes it).
- The WB panel is opaque black, not glass, and covers about 30% of the viewfinder. The selected preset is a teal disc, while EV and the mode switch use other selection colours.
- The mode switch is centred at the top, where the system Camera places nothing, so the eye goes there first.

**Camera: Film strip** (`camera-film-cine500T-filmstrip.png`, `camera-film-expanded.png`)
- Four dashed slots, a mono sentence and three buttons sit over the top third of the viewfinder, exactly where faces are framed in a photobooth shot.
- The disabled Generate (amber check at about 30% opacity) reads as broken rather than "not yet".
- The secondary buttons are outlined rounded squares with a 9 pt radius, a fourth shape family.
- The v4 Film chip plus WB disc sit stacked above the shutter, with no dial. v5.1 F9 fixes this.

**Editor** (`edit-film-tab.png`, `edit-effects-tab.png`, `edit-print-tab.png`, `edit-download-saved.png`)
- The photo takes about 37% of the height. Five full-width sliders take as much space as the photo. v5.1 fixes this with `ToolIconBar` and `EffectScrubber`.
- Film chips wrap 3/3/1 with ragged centring, and stocks are chosen by name only.
- "Hold photo to compare" is a text hint in the top bar. The gesture has no visible control and no VoiceOver action.
- The primary button uses white on mid-teal (3.73:1). Discard is an outlined capsule the same size as the primary, so it has equal weight.
- v5.1 in progress (`worktrees/camera/.../PhotoEditView.swift`): the photo fills the screen and the top bar floats on glass. Good.
  - Remaining risk: an always-dark "darkroom" editor feeding into a cream prompt screen (the surface jump above).

**Prompt / diary** (`review-ok-prompt-mono400.png`)
- The photo is a full-width, unrounded grey band. The prompt is cut short (see §1.6).
- The writing card has a border on cream, so it looks like a form field, not paper.
- About 25% of the screen is empty below the buttons, and Save entry isn't attached to the keyboard.
- "Retake" on this screen is obsolete in v5.1: the photo is already in the Roll.

**Strip screen** (`strip-screen-sliders.png`, `strip-screen-stock-menu.png`, `strip-screen-asShot-*.png`)
- The Close capsule overlaps the strip preview, and the title "Strip" collides with it.
- The action row is clipped at the bottom.
- The swatch circles for paper have no labels (VoiceOver needs them).
- Five sliders plus a toggle in a card push the strip off screen. v5.1 F10 plans a glass sheet; StripView is untouched so far in the worktree.

**Library: Roll** (`library-roll-grid.png`, `library-roll-select.png`, `library-roll-empty.png`)
- The first thumbnail bleeds past the leading safe margin (x = 0). Cells are square while photos are 3:4 or 4:5.
- No day grouping and no count.
- The "Select" glass capsule shows (disabled) on an empty roll.
- The underline segmented tabs below a 34 pt bold "Library" title use about 230 pt before any content.

**Library: Entries** (`diary-list-cine500T.png`)
- Rows show the prompt only. The user's own words, the date/time and the stock are missing.
- "Make today's strip" is a teal-on-teal toolbar capsule at 4.16:1 that looks disabled.
- Content runs under the floating tab bar with no scroll-edge blur.

**Library: Strips** (`library-strips-booth.png`)
- One centred strip about 90 pt wide per row, with the day label under it in tracked caps.
- About 80% of the width is unused, and the strips are too small to read.

**Entry viewer** (`entry-detail-cine500T.png`)
- The response text is missing (or empty in the fixture: check). The prompt is grey italic at low contrast.
- The date is in mono, and there's no stock or print metadata.
- No actions besides ✕, and no way to re-edit the linked Roll photo (v5.1 F5 makes that link live).

**Strip viewer** (`strip-detail-booth.png`)
- Good hero. The ✕ is a dark disc with no glass.
- "TODAY" in tracked teal caps duplicates the date printed on the strip.
- The single CTA is white on mid-teal (contrast), and there's no Share.

**Settings** (`settings-sliders.png`, `wt-restyle/before-settings-*-{light,dark}.png`)
- Five effect sliders inline in the list.
- Section headers are inconsistent with iOS 26 title-case headers.
- Destructive rows are rust (fails in dark).
- The walkthrough bubble covers the content and points to the wrong anchor (`before-settings-top-light.png`).

**Walkthrough** (`wt-restyle/before-settings-top-light.png`)
- A custom white bubble and a hand-drawn arrow, outside the design language.
- It dims the whole screen, and in the captured state it explains Library while on Settings.

**Tab bar** (all)
- A native `TabView` (correct). On iOS 26 it is already the floating glass bar.
- Problems:
  - a global tint that fails on dark;
  - cream and dark appearances switching per tab;
  - no minimise-on-scroll in the Library.

- Keep the camera calm: few visible controls, one corner for depth, and values flashed large in the viewfinder.

## 4. The v5.2 design direction

### 4.1 Principles

1. **The picture is the interface.** Chrome sits on black around the image where there is room, and floats on glass only where there is not.
2. **One of each.**
   - one accent (amber, as a fill);
   - one selection style;
   - one primary action per screen;
   - one control shape family: circle and capsule, plus concentric rounded rects for media.
3. **Darkroom → paper.**
   - Capture and editing are always dark (darkroom).
   - Writing and browsing follow the system appearance (paper in light, darkroom-teal in dark).
   - Transitions between the two are animated, not cut.
4. **Words are serif, numbers are mono digits, everything else is SF.** Full mono only for stock codes and the strip date stamp.
5. **Film is a ritual, not a costume.** Haptics, timing and layout borrow from cameras; textures and fake leather do not.
6. **System first.** TabView, Form, ContentUnavailableView, TipKit, `.glass` button styles and `sensoryFeedback` before anything custom.

### 4.2 Type scale (Dynamic Type)

Add `Shared/Design/FrmrType.swift` (frontend), a `Font` extension built on text styles so everything scales. Serif = the system serif (New York) via `design: .serif`.

**Recommendation:** close the Newsreader OPEN by adopting the system serif permanently. It has full Dynamic Type, no bundling, and is already what ships.

| Role | Token | Definition | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Screen title | `.frmrTitle` | `.title2`, weight `.semibold`, SF | Library/Settings inline titles |
| Prompt | `.frmrPrompt` | `.title3`, `.serif`, italic | Prompt on the writing screen and viewer |
| Reading | `.frmrReading` | `.body`, `.serif` | Entry text, empty-state titles |
| Label | `.frmrLabel` | `.subheadline`, weight `.medium` | Buttons, tool names, list rows |
| Meta | `.frmrMeta` | `.footnote` | Dates, counts, hints |
| Value | `.frmrValue` | `.subheadline.monospacedDigit()`, `.semibold` | Percentages, Kelvin, EV |
| Code | `.frmrCode` | `.caption`, `design: .monospaced`, `.semibold`, tracking 0.5 | Stock codes (C500), edge print |
| HUD | `.frmrHUD` | `.title`, `.monospacedDigit()`, `.semibold` | Large transient value flash in the viewfinder |

**Camera chrome rule:**
- Glyph-only buttons keep a fixed size, but add `.accessibilityShowsLargeContentViewer()` so long-press shows the enlarged label (Apple's pattern for bars).
- Text in chrome is capped with `.dynamicTypeSize(...DynamicTypeSize.accessibility1)`. Every other screen scales fully.

### 4.3 Spacing, radii, sizes

New `Constants+DesignUI.swift` (frontend), namespace `Constants.DesignUI`. Existing per-screen namespaces (`CameraUI`, `PhotoEditUI`, `ScrubberUI`, `LibraryUI`, `RollUI`, `StripUI`, `DetailUI`, `Onboarding`, `EffectSliderUI`) keep their *specific* values but derive from these tokens.

| Token | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `space.xxs/xs/s/m/l/xl/xxl` | 2 / 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 | 4-pt grid; screen side margin = 16 |
| `gutter.contactSheet` | 2 | Roll grid |
| `radius.thumb` | 6 | Thumbnails, dial swatches |
| `radius.media` | 12 | Photos on paper screens, cards |
| `radius.sheet` | 24 | Custom panels (use system sheets where possible) |
| Capsule / Circle | n/a | All buttons; no 9-pt rounded squares |
| `target.min` | 44 | Hard floor; replaces `glassButtonSmallSize 38` |
| `button.glass` | 44 | Icon buttons on glass |
| `button.primaryHeight` | 50 | Capsule primary/secondary |
| `shutter.outer/inner` | 76 / 64 | Unchanged |
| On iOS 26 | `ConcentricRectangle` | For media inside sheets; `RoundedRectangle(radius.media)` fallback |

### 4.4 Colour roles

Add `ThemePalette` roles in `Color+Palette.swift` (frontend). The hex palette is unchanged; what changes is *which hex plays which role*. Every role gets an Increase Contrast variant (`colorSchemeContrast == .increased`).

| Role | Darkroom (camera, editor: always) | Paper light | Paper dark | Contrast (measured) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Rules:**
- Amber is never text and never a thin line on cream (1.59:1).
- Mid-teal `#0A9396` is retired as a fill behind white text (3.73:1). Primary buttons become amber with near-black text.
- **One selection style everywhere:**
  - in darkroom chrome, a filled amber disc or capsule with a near-black glyph (mode switch, WB preset, EV snap);
  - in lists and segmented controls, the system control tinted with `tint`.
- `ContentView`'s `.tint(.appTealDark)` becomes the adaptive `tint`. The camera tab sets `.toolbarColorScheme(.dark, for: .tabBar)` so the bar matches the darkroom.

### 4.5 Glass usage rules

1. **Glass only on the control layer over media:** viewfinder, editor photo and strip hero. Never on list rows, cards, the Library header or paper screens. Use the system bars there, which already get glass on iOS 26.
2. **No glass on glass.** When the WB/EV sheet opens, the WB/EV buttons morph into it (`glassEffectID` in one `GlassEffectContainer`) rather than sitting under it.
3. **Group by function** (HIG):
   - editor top-left [Discard, Retake] = one glass group;
   - top-right [Compare, Download] = another;
   - never one long bar.
4. **Prominent glass = primary only.** `.buttonStyle(.glassProminent).tint(amber)` on iOS 26 for Save to roll / Generate / Keep strip. On iOS 17–25: an amber capsule fill.
5. **Reduce Transparency** (`@Environment(\.accessibilityReduceTransparency)`): `frmrGlass` falls back to `#001219 @ 92%` in darkroom, or `surface` on paper.
6. **Legibility over bright scenes:** glyphs on glass are white with a 1-pt `black @ 25%` shadow on iOS 17–25 (thin material over a bright sky is not enough); iOS 26 glass handles this itself.

**`GlassBackground.swift` upgrade (frontend, S):**
- `frmrGlass(in:prominent:interactive:)`: on iOS 26, maps to `.glassEffect(.regular.tint(...).interactive(), in:)`;
- `FrmrGlassContainer`: wraps `GlassEffectContainer` on iOS 26, a plain `Group` otherwise;
- `frmrGlassID(_:in:)`: no-op before 26;
- the Reduce Transparency branch.

### 4.6 Iconography

- **SF Symbols only**, one weight (`.medium`), `.hierarchical` rendering in darkroom.
- **Sizes:** 17 pt glyph in 44 pt glass buttons, 20 pt in the editor tool bar, 22 pt for the tab bar (system).
- **Editor tool glyphs:** keep the v5.1 set (`film`, `printer`, `sun.max`, `cloud.fog`, `rays`, `circle.dotted`, `circle.dashed.inset.filled`), but add a **value ring** around each effect icon. The ring is `Circle().trim(to: value / 1.5)` in amber, and replaces the v5.1 "modified" dot.
- **Mode switch:** `camera` / `film` (v5.1). The Library tab could use `photo.stack` to say "roll" (could).
- **No text-plus-icon mixing inside one glass group** (HIG).

### 4.7 Motion and haptics

## 6. Signature moments

Each is offline, uses Apple frameworks only, never delays the user artificially, and respects Reduce Motion and a "Haptics" setting.

1. **Film advance** (shutter, S/M, backend `Haptics` + frontend).
   - On capture, a Core Haptics (`CHHapticEngine`) pattern plays:
     - one sharp transient (the shutter);
     - after 120 ms, three soft decreasing transients 40 ms apart (the lever winding on).
   - Visually, the last-photo thumbnail slides in from the shutter, as the frame advances.
   - Fallback when Core Haptics is unsupported: `.impact(.heavy)`.
   - Test on a device: the simulator has no haptics, so this stays "unverified on device" until a human checks it.
2. **Developing reveal** (editor, S).
   - The JPEG preview is shown in a "latent" state: lower contrast, slightly warm, softened.
   - When the RAW render lands (`FilmLookRenderScheduler` already swaps JPEG→RAW), it resolves over 0.6 s with a crossfade between the two `Image`s.
   - It plays **only for the real wait**. If the RAW is already there, nothing plays.
   - Reduce Motion: a plain crossfade.
3. **Contact-sheet Roll with edge print** (Library, M).
   - Each day is a sheet. A thin rebate line under each row prints frame numbers (`1 ▸ 2 ▸ 3`) and the stock code in `.frmrCode`, in the stock's swatch colour at 60%.
   - Dark rebate in both appearances: a contact sheet is a negative-style artefact, and it gives the Library one recognisable motif.
   - Pure SwiftUI `Text` and shapes; no assets.
4. **Stock swatches** (dial and editor, S backend + S frontend).
   - Render a fixed three-patch chart (a skin-ish, a sky-ish and a foliage-ish colour plus a grey ramp, defined in `Constants.ImageProcessing`) through each stock's cube once at launch, as a 40×28 px image cached in `FilmLookCache`.
   - The dial cell then shows *how the stock behaves*, not just its code: Cine 500T's cool shadows, Mono's grey.
   - Deterministic, offline, and a few milliseconds of CoreImage.
5. **Strip print-out** (strip screen, S).
   - After Generate, the strip slides down from under the top edge as if fed out of a booth slot (`.offset` + spring, 0.8 s), with three soft haptic ticks as each frame clears the slot.
   - It settles with a 1° random tilt that straightens on touch.
   - Reduce Motion: a fade.

### 7.5 Open questions for the user

1. **Revisit (editor after every shot):** keep it mandatory, or add a Settings toggle ("Edit after each shot", default On) for fast shooting sessions? Reason: film-camera rhythm. Halide and Apple let you keep shooting.
2. **Camera layout:** dial and shutter on black *below* the 3:4 image on tall phones (unobstructed image), or always floating over the image on glass (bigger-looking viewfinder)?
3. **Revisit (F9 detail):** move the Film strip gallery, Roll and undo into a mini-strip sheet instead of three small buttons on the camera?
4. **Revisit (walkthrough):** replace the coach-mark tour with contextual TipKit tips?
5. **Stock swatches** on the dial: yes, or plain codes?
6. **Contact-sheet Roll:** dark "negative" sheet in both appearances, or paper in light mode?
7. **Strip screen "Archive":** what should it mean (OPEN #6)? This proposal folds Save and Archive into one "Keep strip" primary, with Download and Share as icons.
8. **Serif:** close the Newsreader OPEN by adopting the system serif permanently?
9. **Haptics:** add a Settings toggle for the richer film-advance pattern?
10. **Tab bar (optional revisit):** stay with the tab bar (recommended for now), or explore a camera-root layout after S1 is measured?
